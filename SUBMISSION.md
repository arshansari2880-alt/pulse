## How to review (2 minutes)

1. Land on the map. You are in Koregaon Park at 8:41 PM. Pulse rings = aliveness.
2. Switch to **Saturday**. Camera moves to FC Road. Pins collapse into numbered blooms. Click a bloom, then a pin.
3. Switch to **Late**. The city goes quiet on purpose. Widen if you want the faint remainder.
4. Filter to **Table**. Other types stay as ghosts so the street does not jump.
